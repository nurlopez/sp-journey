SUPERPENGUIN — A LITTLE FURTHER
Reviewed version 1, October 2026

Open index.html in a desktop browser, or use the website address after GitHub Pages is enabled.
Invitation code: SUP3RP3NGU1N

Arrow keys: move. Space or Up: jump.
Enter or Esc: close a story caption. Esc during play: pause. M: sound. N: memories.
Memories lets you reread passed stories and notifications at your own pace.

Each playable teammate has an original instrumental music loop.
Music starts with the game. Pause stops the music. M mutes music and effects.
Change player in the pause menu keeps your place and pages and changes the music.
Visit a port lets you jump to another chapter while keeping your player and collected pages.

No installation, no internet and no account needed.
Use a desktop/laptop window at least 900 pixels wide.
Falling never removes pages. All pages are optional.
Each new visit starts at the beginning with an empty backpack.
Keep this window open to continue your current journey. Esc pauses the game.

The invitation screen is a playful gate, not encryption.
Anyone with this file can read its content. Share it only with the team.
This is an early souvenir prototype, ready to play and refine.
